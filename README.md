🎵 Music Store SQL Analysis
A deep dive into customer behavior, revenue insights, and music trends across countries using SQL queries.

📁 Files & Datasets Used
Database: Chinook Database (music store schema)

Tables Queried:

Employee

Customer

Invoice

Invoice_Line

Track

Album

Artist

Genre

Tool Used: SQL Editor (e.g., MySQL, SQLite, etc.)

🔹 Beginner Level Insights
👔 Who's the Senior Most Employee?

Query: Sort employees by job level (descending), and pick the top one.

Insight: The senior-most employee holds the highest level in the company hierarchy.

🌍 Which Countries Have the Most Invoices?

Query: Count invoices by billing country.

Insight: Top purchasing countries indicate strong markets.

💸 Top 3 Invoice Totals

Query: Retrieve the top 3 highest invoices.

Insight: These highlight the biggest individual purchases made.

🏙️ Which City Brings in the Most Revenue?

Query: Sum of invoice totals by billing city.

Insight: The city with the highest sales might be ideal for promotions or a music festival.

🌟 Who’s the Best Customer?

Query: Customer who has spent the most overall.

Insight: This customer is the biggest spender and most valuable.

🔸 Moderate Level Insights
🎧 Rock Music Lovers – Who Are They?

Query: Identify customers who purchased Rock genre tracks.

Insight: Targeted marketing can focus on Rock fans based on this list.

🎸 Top Rock Artists by Track Count

Query: Top 10 artists with the most rock songs.

Insight: These are the most prolific rock contributors.

⏱️ Longest Tracks Above Average Length

Query: Tracks longer than the average song length.

Insight: Could be used for curated playlists or premium listening experiences.

🔺 Advanced Level Insights
🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Who Spends the Most on the Top Artist?

Query: Identify which customers spent the most on the best-selling artist.

Insight: High-value fans who are loyal to a specific artist.

🌐 Most Popular Genre by Country

Query: Determine the top genre per country based on purchase quantity.

Insight: Helps personalize offerings based on regional taste.

🌍 Top Spending Customer per Country

Query: Highest spender per country and how much they spent.

Insight: Enables country-level customer engagement strategies.
